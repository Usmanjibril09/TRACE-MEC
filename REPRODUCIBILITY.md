# Reproducibility and scope

This code-only distribution contains implementation, configurations and selected final CSV summaries. It excludes raw data, environments, checkpoints, episode-level predictions, internal notes, conversations, editorial records, private Git history and development reports.

## Supported public workflow

Follow README.md to obtain the two datasets and prepare Train/Validation/Test inputs. release.py verifies distributed source checksums and supports primary dry-run, seed-0 Test and seeds 1–4. Other original drivers retain historical commit guards and require adaptation before use outside the original research checkout. We do not claim complete one-command reproduction of every robustness analysis. Full figure/uncertainty regeneration requires full episode outputs, not just the supplied CSV summaries. Exact dependencies are recorded, but installation has not been retested on every platform. No full experiment was rerun during packaging.

## Scientific interpretation

The final method combines positive-evidence filtering, strongest-first transfer, a monotone destination belief and simulated position-consistency verification. Non-incriminating injection immunity and fault-free equal-size ordering optimality have scoped assumptions. Worst-case empirical risk is over the evaluated adversary menu, not all possible attacks.

Probe observations are synthesized; a deployed covert challenge protocol, missing-response handling and joint transfer/probe scheduling were not validated. VOI ranking and recalibration use label-free evaluation batches, not causal streaming adaptation. PQC evaluation changes item byte size for ML-DSA-44 overhead; it does not implement signing or verification.

Development used Validation before the final design was frozen. Primary Test configurations were evaluated once per seed; separate robustness/extension configurations followed recorded amendments. The position-attack extension omitted its planned Validation evaluation. The hypothesis that larger signatures would degrade TRACE-MEC less than Severity/LSTM in average attack loss was not supported. Primary Validation VOI targeting was not significant. Original selection-only designs failed their development criteria; the final framework's worst-case improvement is chiefly attributed to the probe and robust belief. OOSM reconciliation and a joint transfer/access-control optimizer are not implemented contributions.
